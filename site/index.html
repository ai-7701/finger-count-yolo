How it works

1

Find the hand

MediaPipe's hand landmarker locates 21 points on your hand, every frame.

2

Crop and resize

The bounding box around those points is cropped with 25% padding and resized to 224×224.

3

Classify

A YOLO11n classifier outputs six scores; the highest one is the finger count.

The model rarely confuses two neighbouring counts; when it does, 5 is read as 4 (11 of 147 cases) and 4 as 3 (6 of 142).

What it does not do

  • One hand only. Two hands in frame and it counts whichever it finds first.
  • Raised fingers, not gestures. A thumbs-up reads as 1; it is not sign language.
  • Needs decent light. Very dim rooms defeat the hand detector before the classifier ever runs.
  • Best with fingers apart. A tight fist or overlapping fingers can be read one count off.
